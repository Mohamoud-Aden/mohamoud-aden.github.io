About Me

I am Mohamoud Aden Jama, an Accounting and Finance professional, lecturer, and researcher based in Erigavo, Somaliland. I hold a Bachelor's Degree and a Master's Degree in Accounting and Finance.


My academic and professional interests include Accounting Information Systems, financial performance, SME finance, financial reporting, auditing, taxation, budgeting, cash management, and financial management.


I am interested in research that contributes to improved financial practices, economic growth, innovation, and sustainable development.
